# Credits

- The lower bound gap >= c / (n^3 C_PI) for coordinate hit-and-run on convex bodies containing a unit ball, the resulting mixing bound O(n^3 C_PI log(M/eps)), and the statement that this bound is conjectured to be asymptotically tight: Y. Kook and S. S. Vempala, arXiv:2608.16878, Theorem 1.1 and Section 1 [KV]. Known; used in the introduction and in the orders of the gap and of C_PI on K_n.
- Coordinate hit-and-run as the Gibbs sampler: V. F. Turchin (1971) [Turchin]. Known.
- Polynomial mixing of coordinate hit-and-run, and a sheared cylinder of diameter D containing a unit ball on which its conductance is O(1/(n^{3/2} D)): A. Laddha and S. Vempala, Discrete Comput. Geom. 70 (2023), Theorem 5 [LV20]. Known; with gap <= 2 phi and C_PI <= diam^2/pi^2 it gives gap C_PI = O(D n^{-3/2}) only.
- Warm-start mixing bounds between the l_infinity ball and its dilation: H. Narayanan and P. Srivastava, Combin. Probab. Comput. 31 (2022) [NS20]. Known.
- Mixing with logarithmic dependence on the warmness: H. Narayanan, A. Rajaraman and P. Srivastava, Probab. Theory Related Fields 191 (2025) 1169-1232 [NRS]. Known.
- l_0 isoperimetry for convex bodies and the resulting coordinate hit-and-run bounds: M. Fernandez, arXiv:2608.27854 [Fernandez]. Known.
- Hit-and-run mixing in terms of the outer radius: L. Lovász and S. Vempala, SIAM J. Comput. 35 (2006) [LV06]. Known.
- The Poincaré constant upper bound C_PI <= diam^2 / pi^2 for convex domains: L. E. Payne and H. F. Weinberger, Arch. Rational Mech. Anal. 5 (1960) [PW], with a corrected proof by M. Bebendorf, Z. Anal. Anwend. 22 (2003) 751-756 [Beb], and in the form of L. Esposito, C. Nitsch and C. Trombetti, J. Convex Anal. 20 (2013) 253-264, equation (1.4) and Theorem 1.1 at p = 2 [ENT]. Known.
- The identity E_K(f,f) = mean over i of E Var(f | X_{-i}) for a random-scan Gibbs sampler (Lemma 2.1). Known; proof included.

New in this paper: the polytopes K_n, the bounds on C_PI and on the coordinate hit-and-run gap for K_n, the conclusion that the factor n^3 in the Kook-Vempala gap bound is sharp, and the mixing-time lower bound of Corollary 1.2, which shows the same for their mixing bound.
